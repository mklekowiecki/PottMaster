# Catalog of Popular Ceramic Glazes

Reference document – overview of commonly used ceramic glazes  
with essential technical parameters for studio and workshop use.

---

## 1. Glaze Classification by Firing Temperature

| Glaze Type | Temperature Range | Pyrometric Cone | Typical Use |
|-----------|------------------|------------------|-------------|
| Low-fire | 700–1080 °C | Cone 06–02 | Decorative ceramics |
| Mid-fire | 1080–1230 °C | Cone 4–6 | Functional ware |
| High-fire | 1230–1450 °C | Cone 8–11 | Stoneware, reduction |

## 6. Key Technical Parameters

- Firing temperature range
- Pyrometric cone
- Surface finish: gloss / matte / satin
- Transparency: transparent / semi-opaque / opaque
- Food safety and functional use compatibility
- Reactivity and special effects
- Coefficient of thermal expansion (COE)

---

## 7. Practical Notes

- Always test glazes on the intended clay body
- Results depend on application thickness
- Layering significantly affects final appearance
- Refer to manufacturer technical data sheets
